MARS Lab

Robotic manipulation in unstructured environments demands high resolution tactile feedback analogous to human skin. Within the MARS Lab subgroup under Md Rakibul Islam Prince, our research focuses on designing, fabricating, and evaluating miniaturized optical and capacitive tactile sensing skins (PhoTac, HueTac, and TacSkin) integrated onto dynamic robotic end-effectors. We operate within an Agile framework, leveraging weekly iterations, mob design sessions, and collaborative hardware sprints.

Design and Development

Personal 6-DOF Robot

A personal project. I designed and built a 6-joint robot arm from scratch, including the CAD model, the kinematics, and a 3D animation.

  • 6 revolute joints, with a waist, shoulder, elbow, and spherical wrist
  • Base height 200 mm, upper arm 250 mm, forearm reach 250 mm, tool offset 80 mm
  • Reach: 580 mm from the shoulder
  • Animation: 30 seconds, interactive orbit, Play and Loop, with circle, triangle, star, and umbrella shapes drawn by the tool tip

I used MATLAB for the kinematics behind this robot: forward kinematics from the Modified Denavit-Hartenberg parameters, numerical inverse kinematics, cubic trajectory planning for circle, square, and sine paths, Jacobian velocity and torque analysis, and a 15,000 sample Monte Carlo workspace check. The paths all tracked with 0.000 mm error in MATLAB, and the workspace check set the 580 mm reach from the shoulder used to place the paths.
